How we help you move forward

Six ways we work with clients, from finding the opportunity to running what we build. Each is described in full below.

Service 01

Artificial Intelligence

We help you identify where AI creates real value, govern it responsibly, and put it to work with measurable results. Teams using our AI tooling have reported productivity gains of 30% or more.

AI Strategy

Roadmaps built around governance, data management and scalability, focused on the opportunities with the highest real-world payoff.

Readiness Assessment

An honest look at your infrastructure, data, talent and culture, so you know what needs to be true before you invest.

Build & Deploy

Technology selection, custom development, integration, testing and deployment, with support that continues after go-live.

Ethics & Governance

Frameworks that keep model bias understood and managed, protect privacy, and build stakeholder trust.

In practice

DigiBlu Engage, our email virtual agent, automates up to 75% of routine email handling - classification, information-gathering, policy checks and drafted responses.

Discuss this service
Service 02

Opportunity Discovery

We assess where you stand today, surface the highest-impact opportunities, and build the case for change - combining traditional process mapping with AI-enabled discovery.

Opportunity Assessment

Diagnoses known and unknown issues across processes, teams and technology, backed by real-life data rather than assumption.

Task Mining

Real usage data on how your teams interact with business applications - particularly valuable where SLA compliance and standardisation matter, such as shared services.

Process Mining

End-to-end analysis of how activities, systems and stakeholders connect across a complex process, not just one step of it.

What you get

A clearer picture of your current operating model, pain points ranked by business impact, and a prioritised improvement list with future-state designs.

Discuss this service
Service 03

Process Excellence

We simplify the people, processes, and technology behind your operations into one coherent, well-run system, drawing on Lean Six Sigma, Total Quality Management and Business Process Reengineering as the toolkit, not the goal.

Design & Assess

We start by understanding your goals and constraints, then map what is actually happening today against KPIs, workshops and frontline feedback.

Improve & Implement

Workflows are redesigned around the changes that matter, then rolled out with a prioritised plan and real change management.

What we optimise for

Efficiency, effectiveness, standardisation, customer satisfaction and continuous improvement, reviewed on a cycle rather than left to drift.

Process Documentation

Process maps, standard operating procedures and training material that make the new way of working stick, not just launch.

Discuss this service
Service 04

Digital Solutions

We design, build, and deploy tailored digital and automation solutions in weeks, with support that continues after launch.

Agentic AI

AI agents that handle routine and complex customer queries around the clock, freeing your team for the conversations that need a person.

Robotic Process Automation

Software robots that take on repetitive, rules-based tasks, cutting errors and cost with components you can reuse elsewhere.

Systems Integration

Modern APIs and legacy RPA working together so your systems talk to each other instead of duplicating work.

Power Platform & Web Apps

Low-code business applications and web forms that streamline data collection and open the door to further automation.

Business Intelligence & UX

Raw data turned into a holistic view for decision-making, delivered through interfaces people actually want to use.

Discuss this service
Service 05

Target Operating Model

We shape an operating model that aligns your people, processes, and technology to what the business actually needs, built around four pillars: process, technology, people and organisational design.

Process & Technology

Objective-led process design and technology selection, chosen for what the business needs rather than what is fashionable.

People & Culture

Role definitions, skills and change management that make the new model something your team can actually run.

Organisational Design

Structure, RACI mapping and KPIs, including centralised-versus-decentralised and offshore-versus-in-house decisions.

Specialist support

Shared services set-up, M&A integration, and change management for when the model has to shift fast.

Discuss this service
Service 06

Managed Services

We keep your systems sharp as needs evolve, with ongoing updates that protect the value you have already built through managed support and continuous improvement.

Managed support

Round-the-clock support from developers and process controllers who respond fast when something breaks.

Continuous Improvement

Ongoing modifications from our developers and analysts as the business around the automation keeps changing.

Monitoring, your way

Proactive monitoring that flags issues automatically, or a reactive model where you request enhancements as you need them.

Discuss this service

Experienced practitioners with client, technology, and consultancy backgrounds, working as pragmatic, technology-agnostic partners focused on speed to value.
