## Overview

Safari Destinations is the leading destination management company in Botswana and Zimbabwe. Recognising the opportunity to transform how its business operates, it selected DigiBlu as its AI transformation partner: a partnership that combines AI, automation, data and digital solutions, with DigiBlu supporting the full transformation lifecycle from identifying and assessing opportunities through delivery, managed service and continuous improvement.

## The problem

Several operational processes relied heavily on email, spreadsheets, manual data entry and employee knowledge. Supplier and traveller information needed significant manual handling, and critical management reports depended on repetitive Excel-based preparation from data held across different systems. Safari Destinations wanted to explore the potential of AI while keeping investment focused on practical opportunities with genuine business value, and needed a partner able to identify the right opportunities and turn them into scalable, sustainable solutions.

## What we did

DigiBlu worked with Safari Destinations to identify, prioritise and deliver a portfolio of transformation initiatives. We digitised the supplier rates process with structured online data capture and automated document management, and transformed the client (traveller) information process through digital forms, automated workflows and consolidated communications. We implemented business intelligence and automated management reporting in place of manually maintained Excel reports, improving access to operational information, alongside enhancements to performance management, and explored further AI opportunities in areas including pricing analysis and supplier proforma invoice processing and matching. DigiBlu continues to support Safari Destinations through an ongoing managed service and continuous improvement model, maintaining the existing solutions while identifying enhancements and new transformation opportunities.

## Outcome

Safari Destinations now has a more automated, data-driven and scalable approach to its operations. Manual administration has been reduced, key processes have moved away from email and spreadsheets, and management information is more consistent and accessible. Through the ongoing managed service and continuous improvement model it also has a sustainable way to evolve its existing solutions and pursue new AI opportunities as the business and the technology landscape develop, with DigiBlu acting as a long-term transformation partner that combines consulting and technical delivery to turn emerging technology into practical business value.
