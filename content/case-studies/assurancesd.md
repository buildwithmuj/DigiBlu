## Overview

AssuranceSD is a leading Financial Management Services provider, administering self-directed programs for individuals with long-term care needs - from onboarding and hiring through payroll and billing - across multiple US states.

## The problem

The government funding application process was heavily paper-based, causing delays, confusion and a heavy administrative load. It was especially hard on elderly and disabled applicants. Manual data entry drove frequent resubmissions, delays cost the program sponsor revenue, and staff were tied up in low-value repetitive work.

## What we did

We built a secure, stateful web application letting users authenticate, complete and amend applications online, removing paper forms entirely. Forms were digitised in Jotform with validation rules and required fields to improve accuracy, and Jotform Sign was integrated so applicants and authorised representatives could sign electronically for compliant multi-signatory submissions. Repeated design iterations focused on accessibility for applicants with physical or mental disabilities.

## Outcome

Applicants get a more intuitive, supportive process with fewer errors and faster approvals. Automated data flow between systems cut manual intervention, freeing staff for personalised participant support. Digital submissions gave AssuranceSD and its clients real-time insight into applicant demographics and process bottlenecks, and the shift off paper cut administrative costs.
