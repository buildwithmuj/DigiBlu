## Overview

OVO, one of the UK’s top energy providers, was midway through migrating the SSE customer base onto the OVO brand and technology platform.

## The problem

Rising energy prices and the cost-of-living crisis drove a surge in calls to the contact centre. Customers wanted to reset direct debit payments and clear outstanding debt, but the volume meant long waits and poor experience - fixable only by hiring more staff.

## What we did

With internal developers committed to the migration, DigiBlu built virtual agents able to automate even complex conversations. Direct Debit Reviews reproduce the contact-centre conversation across QR codes, URLs and WhatsApp, guiding customers through the options available to them from live account data. Collections presents customers with recommended ways to settle a balance - payment plans, alternative meter options - so arrears can be resolved without an uncomfortable phone call.

## Outcome

Calls and chats were successfully deflected from agents, with every digital interaction fully compliant with policy. Cost per transaction fell to under half that of a human-handled case, driving a forecast ROI of 350%. Engaging customers early also reduces defaults and cancelled direct debits, protecting revenue.
