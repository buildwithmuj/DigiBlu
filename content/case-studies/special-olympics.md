## Overview

Special Olympics is a global grassroots movement bringing community programmes and competition to more than 4.5 million children and adults with intellectual disabilities across 170 countries. In Canada, over 41,000 people take part, supported by local sport clubs and more than 17,500 volunteers.

## The problem

Provincial and territorial Chapters apply to Special Olympics Canada (SOC) for programme funding each year. The process ran on Microsoft Excel and long email chains, losing version control and giving everyone a poor experience. SOC already used Jotform elsewhere, but this data was more complex - needing careful analysis of the future-state workflow and custom widgets.

## What we did

Jotform introduced SOC to DigiBlu as an Elite Partner experienced in complex custom builds. A discovery workshop with SOC subject-matter experts mapped the workflow and found opportunities to improve it. We built custom widgets alongside a portal where SOC and the Chapters collaborate on grant applications, document budget expenditure and track performance against targets.

## Outcome

The portal gives both SOC and the Chapters a clear experience for submitting applications and updating records through the year. Custom widgets handle standardisation and automation, and structuring a yearly record per Chapter eliminated the version-control problem entirely - leaving everyone free to focus on the events that change thousands of lives.
