## Overview

Cedar Creek Church is a gospel-centred church in Aiken, South Carolina. Alongside its weekly services it runs ministries, groups, events and community support, and its team coordinates the operational requests behind them - payments, facilities, room bookings, events and maintenance work.

## The problem

Several processes relied on paper, spreadsheets and other manual methods, so information was captured inconsistently and requests did not always follow the right review and approval route. Coordination was heavy and teams had little visibility of status. The church wanted a single digital forms platform its own team could manage, and found DigiBlu through Jotform’s partner network.

## What we did

We ran process workshops with the church’s stakeholders to validate requirements and agree the best future process for each type of request, confirming Jotform was well suited. We then designed and configured six Jotform solutions - payment requests, expense pre-approvals, facilities improvements, room requests, event requests and maintenance work orders - with validation, conditional logic, automated notifications and approval workflows, plus integrations for calendar scheduling and collaboration. Designed, tested and launched within three weeks, with documentation and training so the team can manage the forms independently.

## Outcome

Cedar Creek Church now has a standardised digital foundation for its operational requests. Information is captured consistently at submission, automated workflows carry each request through the right review and approval stages, and every action is recorded, giving clear, auditable processes with greater accountability and visibility. Six end-to-end digital workflows replace paper, spreadsheet and email processes, freeing teams to spend more time supporting the church and its community.
