## Overview

Our client is one of Europe’s largest healthcare workforce solutions providers, deploying over 30,000 highly trained professionals each week across healthcare, social care and life sciences.

## The problem

In an industry already stretched for resources, candidate onboarding had become a bottleneck. The manual system - PDF forms, emails and phone calls - needed 20 staff to manage applications and averaged 90 days from application to onboarding, causing frustration, high dropout rates and delayed placements at critical facilities.

## What we did

We combined Jotform automation with Robotic Process Automation. Status-driven forms trigger automatically at each onboarding step, cutting manual handovers and errors. Real-time validation lets candidates submit complete, accurate information first time. Bots update business systems with candidate details, documents and background checks, handle NMC and DBS checks and schedule virtual interviews. Custom dashboards pull form data into a warehouse and feed Power BI for live leadership insight.

## Outcome

Average onboarding fell from 90 days to 45, enabling faster placements and quicker candidate payments. Dropout rates dropped, automated validation cut data errors and improved compliance, and the operations team doubled candidate outreach by spending less time on admin. The approach was then rolled out across other brands in the client’s portfolio.
