## Overview

Our client is a US-based agency supporting key projects for a major US city, focused on urgent responses to natural disasters and the refugee crisis - work that typically demands new processes and technology at very short notice.

## The problem

Humanitarian relief centers can be hectic: high footfall, language barriers, heightened emotion and a great deal of uncertainty. Structure and efficiency are critical to making people feel safe, gathering the information that matters and getting them the support they need.

## What we did

We launched a HIPAA-compliant form at short notice so support staff could capture intake information on tablets. Two-way integration with the core database ensured data was captured against the correct individual and made its way onto their record. Given the volume of arrivals and the range of complex needs, the form was integrated with a Power BI dashboard so management could see, in real time, whether enough support was available.

## Outcome

Within a week the client was documenting intake through the digital solution, bringing structure to the administrative process and a better experience for arrivals. Hours per day previously spent compiling and presenting management information were eliminated, freeing that time to interrogate the data and keep improving the services being offered.
