## 1. Introduction

This statement relates to actions and activities during the 2024 financial and calendar year.
DigiBlu UK Limited is exempt from the Modern Slavery Act 2015, but voluntarily complies with its provisions to ensure there is no slavery or human trafficking in Our own operations or Our supply chain. We assess and manage this risk through Our organisation structure and supply chains, related policies, due diligence processes, risk assessment and management, key performance indicators, and staff training.
We all have a duty to be alert to risks, however small, and management provides staff with a supportive environment for reporting them.

## 2. Organisation Structure and Supply Chains

DigiBlu is a company registered in England and Wales, delivering services through directly employed staff and third-party suppliers based principally in the UK, USA, UAE, Europe and South Africa. DigiBlu’s registered office is in Chelmsford, UK.
Our supply chain includes robotic process automation (RPA) and other technology suppliers providing cloud hosting, cloud automation software, and cloud support services to public and private sector clients. Main supply chain partners include Blue Prism, Thoughtonomy, AntWorks, TrustPortal, CLEVVA and SoftTools.

## 3. Policies in Relation to Slavery and Human Trafficking

DigiBlu confirms its zero-tolerance position on modern slavery, reflecting a commitment to acting ethically and with integrity in all business relationships, with effective systems and controls to ensure there is no slavery or human trafficking anywhere in Our supply chain. Clear guidelines are in place, aligned with UK Government best practice, for confidentially raising any identified wrongdoing - all reports are fully investigated with appropriate remedial action.

## 4. Due Diligence Process

Each supplier relationship has an assigned relationship manager, accountable for ensuring robust processes are in place to avoid slavery and human trafficking. Supplier review meetings verify that each supplier maintains systematic vigilance against these risks, both in its own organisation and those of its suppliers.

## 5. Risk Assessment and Management

DigiBlu prioritises scrutiny of Our own and supplier locations that pose an increased likelihood of slavery or human trafficking, with increased frequency of engagement at those locations.

## 6. Key Performance Indicators

DigiBlu is working towards KPIs verifying that 100% of new employees are appraised of this statement, that employees are re-appraised on each employment anniversary, and that compliance is verified at each supplier relationship meeting.

## 7. Training on Modern Slavery and Trafficking

Employees complete initial induction and annual refresher training on DigiBlu’s Modern Slavery and Human Trafficking Policy, and are encouraged to be vigilant and escalate any concerns to their line manager or, if no action is taken, via the Company’s Whistleblowing Policy.

## 8. DigiBlu’s Commitment

The Board and all DigiBlu employees are committed to ensuring there is no slavery, servitude, forced or compulsory labour, abuse of power over vulnerable individuals, human trafficking, or any other form of exploitation, as contemplated by the Modern Slavery Act 2015, in Our own organisation or Our supply chain.
This statement was approved by the Board of DigiBlu UK Limited.
