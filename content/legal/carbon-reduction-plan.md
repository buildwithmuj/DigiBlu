## Overview

This Carbon Reduction Plan was produced by DigiBlu UK Limited (company registration no. 12015792; registered office Steeple House, Suite 3 Floor One, Church Lane, Chelmsford, Essex, CM1 1NH) on 1 January 2026.

## 1. Introduction

DigiBlu is committed to reducing its carbon emissions over time, with the objective of achieving Net Zero carbon emissions by 31 December 2050.
This pledge supports the UK Government’s requirement that, from 30 September 2022, in-scope organisations - including suppliers to Crown Commercial Service via the G-Cloud Framework, such as DigiBlu - monitor their carbon emissions and comply with carbon reduction targets working toward Net Zero greenhouse gas emissions by 31 December 2050.
DigiBlu is pleased to report a 12.5% reduction in the Tonnes of Carbon Dioxide (tCO2e) and other greenhouse gases generated by the business during 2025, achieved through determined effort by DigiBlu colleagues - a 30% cumulative reduction in GHG against the 2022 baseline year.

## 2. Baseline Emissions Footprint

The first step in developing a carbon reduction target was establishing a Baseline Emissions record - the GHG produced directly or indirectly through DigiBlu’s trading in 2022, before any reduction strategies were introduced. The 2022 baseline is the reference point against which emissions reduction is measured.

## 3. Actual tCO2e Emissions During 2025

DigiBlu targets Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions reductions aligned with the Government’s goal of limiting global temperature rise to 1.5°C above pre-industrial levels.
Scope 1 covers direct emissions from the business’s own operations; Scope 2 covers indirect emissions from purchased electricity; Scope 3 covers indirect emissions from assets DigiBlu does not directly own - for example those created by an outsourced service provider. Together these make up DigiBlu’s Corporate Carbon Footprint.
The principal sources of emissions in a professional services business like DigiBlu are business travel, employee commuting, remote working, waste generated in operations, and fuel and energy related activities.
Forecast GHG emissions for the year ending 31 December 2025 reduced to 1.12 tCO2e per colleague, with total forecast emissions of 33.71 tCO2e for the year.

## 4. Emissions Reduction Target

DigiBlu has set a global ambition to be carbon neutral by the end of 2050, and will update this plan as new carbon reduction projects are implemented.
tCO2e generated per worker has reduced by 30% compared with the 2022 baseline. During 2026, DigiBlu will work toward a further 10% reduction in the GHG generated by the business.

## 5. Carbon Reduction Projects

All four of DigiBlu’s executive directors have now acquired an electric vehicle for business and personal mileage.
DigiBlu remains committed to achieving carbon net zero by the end of 2030, and will target: further reducing international air travel by the end of 2026 against a 2022 baseline, and encouraging mass transport or electric vehicles for individual journeys; reducing office electricity use and procuring 100% renewable energy for what remains; structuring electricity supply contracts (including virtual power purchase agreements) to put more electricity into national grids than DigiBlu consumes; giving DigiBlu teams tools to calculate and reduce the carbon emitted on client work; using nature-based solutions and carbon-reduction technologies to remove or offset more carbon than DigiBlu emits each year; investing in services that help clients profitably decarbonise their own businesses; and requiring third-party suppliers representing 75% of DigiBlu’s total third-party spend to align with its net zero strategy by 2050.

## 6. Declaration and Sign Off

This plan was completed in accordance with PPN 06/21 and its associated guidance and reporting standard for Carbon Reduction Plans. Emissions are reported using the GHG Reporting Protocol corporate standard and UK Government emission conversion factors; Scope 1 and 2 emissions follow Streamlined Energy and Carbon Reporting (SECR) requirements, and the required subset of Scope 3 emissions follows the Corporate Value Chain (Scope 3) Standard.
This plan was reviewed and signed off by the Board of Directors on behalf of DigiBlu UK Limited.
