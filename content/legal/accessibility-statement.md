## Our commitment

This statement applies to the website at www.digiblu.com, which is run by DigiBlu UK Limited. We want as many people as possible to be able to use it. That means you should be able to read it in a light or a dark colour scheme, zoom in to 400% without text spilling off the screen, move through every page using only a keyboard, and read the content in a logical order with headings and labels in place. We also keep the language as plain as we can.

## How accessible this website is

We have measured the site against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA in the following areas, and it meets the standard in each: text contrast in both colour schemes; no horizontal scrolling at screen widths from 320 pixels upwards; tap and click targets of at least 24 pixels; the menu, the cookie choices and the contact form can be used from the keyboard, and the menu returns you to where you were when it closes; a skip link takes keyboard users straight to the content; and every animation stops when your device has its reduce-motion setting switched on.

## What we know is not fully accessible

The two moving strips on the home page (our accreditations and our client logos) pause when you hover over them and stop entirely if your reduce-motion setting is on, but they do not yet have a pause button. We have not yet tested the site with screen-reader software, so some labels and announcements may be less clear than we intend. The site has not yet been independently audited. We are working through these and will update this statement as they are resolved.

## Feedback and contact information

If you find a problem that is not listed here, or you need any content from this site in a different format, please tell us. You can use the contact form on this site (Get in touch, on every page), or email us at vic.gysin@digiblu.com. Please describe the problem, the page it is on, and the browser or assistive technology you were using, and we will respond as quickly as we can.

## Technical information about this website

DigiBlu UK Limited is a private company, so the Public Sector Bodies (Websites and Mobile Applications) (No. 2) Accessibility Regulations 2018 do not apply to this website. We publish this statement voluntarily, in the same form those regulations ask of public bodies, because we work with public sector clients and hold ourselves to the same standard. We believe this website is partially compliant with WCAG 2.2 level AA: the areas listed above have been tested and meet the standard, and the limitations listed above have not yet been resolved or tested.

## How we tested this website

The measurements above were made on 9, 10 and 11 September 2026 during the build of this version of the site, by DigiBlu, using automated checks of the rendered pages (colour contrast computed from the colours actually displayed in both colour schemes, layout checked at screen widths of 320, 375, 390, 740, 1440 and 1895 pixels, and target sizes measured on every control) together with a keyboard walk through every page. They were repeated on 21 September 2026, after the pop-up dialogs were replaced by pages of their own. No third party has audited the site yet.

## Preparation of this statement

This statement was prepared on 10 September 2026 and updated on 11 September 2026, when the services, team, accreditations, case-studies and contact pages were added and checked with the same automated tests, and on 21 September 2026, when the pop-up dialogs were replaced by those pages, the navigation was fixed to the top of every page and the cookie banner was moved and made smaller. It will be reviewed when the site changes materially and at least once a year.
