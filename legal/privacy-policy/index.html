Privacy and Cookies Policy

Last updated 24 September 2026.

Introduction

This Privacy Policy is published by DigiBlu UK Limited (company registration no. 12015792; registered office C/O CBHC Limited, Steeple House, Suite 3 First Floor, Church Lane, Chelmsford, CM1 1NH, UK).
This notice explains how We collect, store and process any information that We collect from You, or that You provide to Us - both information that could identify You (“personal information”) and information that could not.
We are committed to protecting Your privacy and the confidentiality of Your personal information. Our policy complies with the Data Protection Act 2018 and the UK GDPR.
Except as set out below, We do not share, sell, or disclose to a third party any information collected through Our website.

1. Data Protection Officer

We have appointed David Williams as Our Data Protection and GDPR Officer. If You have any questions about this Privacy Policy, including any requests to exercise Your legal rights, please contact him at privacy@digiblu.com.

2. Data We Process

Identity data: first name, last name, title, date of birth, and other identifiers You may have provided.
Contact information: billing address, delivery address, email address, telephone numbers and any other information given for the purpose of communication or meeting.
Technical data: Your IP address, browser type and version, time zone setting and location, browser plug-in types and versions, operating system and platform, and other technology on the devices You use to access Our website.
Marketing data: Your preferences in receiving marketing from Us, communication preferences, and Your responses and actions in relation to Our services.
We may also aggregate anonymous data - such as statistical or demographic data - for any purpose. If aggregated data is combined with Your personal information so that it can identify You, We treat the combined data as personal information.

3. Special Personal Information

Special personal information is data about Your race or ethnicity, religious or philosophical beliefs, personal preferences, political opinions, trade union membership, health, and genetic or biometric data. We do not collect any special personal information about You.

4. If You Do Not Provide Personal Information We Need

Where We need to collect personal data by law, or under the terms of a contract with You, Your employer, or a third party for whom We are providing services You have requested, and You fail to provide that data, We may not be able to perform that contract. In that case We may have to stop providing a service, and We will notify You of this at the time.

5. Information We Process Because We Have a Contractual Obligation With You

When You or Your employer create an account on Our website, buy a product or service from Us, or otherwise agree to Our terms and conditions, a contract is formed between You and Us. To carry out Our obligations under that contract We must process the information You give Us - to verify Your identity for security purposes, sell products to You, provide You with Our services, and provide suggestions and advice on products, services and how to get the most from Our website.
We continue to process this information until the contract between Us ends or is terminated by either party under its terms.

6. Information We Process With Your Consent

Through actions such as browsing Our website or asking Us for more information about Our business, products and services, You provide Your consent for Us to process information that may be personal information. Wherever possible We aim to obtain Your explicit consent, for example by asking You to agree to Our use of cookies.
If You have given Us explicit permission, We may from time to time pass Your name and contact information to third parties with whom We provide products and services where You have expressed interest in obtaining them.
We continue to process Your information on this basis until You withdraw Your consent via Our “Unsubscribe” function - though doing so may limit Your ability to use Our website or services further.

7. Information We Process for the Purposes of Legitimate Interests

We may process information on the basis of a legitimate interest to You, Your employer, a prospective employer, or Us - for example, for record-keeping and proper administration of DigiBlu; responding to unsolicited communication from You; protecting and asserting the legal rights of any party; insuring against or obtaining professional advice to manage DigiBlu’s risks; and protecting Your interests where We believe We have a duty to do so.
Before processing on this basis We consider whether the same objective could be achieved another way, whether processing (or not processing) could cause You harm, and whether You would reasonably expect Us to process Your data.

8. Information We Process Because We Have a Legal Obligation

Sometimes We must process Your information to comply with a statutory obligation - for example, providing information to legal authorities if they request it with proper authorisation, such as a search warrant or court order.

9. Communicating With Us

When You contact Us - by telephone, through Our website, or by email - We collect the data You give Us in order to reply. We record Your request and Our reply to improve DigiBlu’s efficiency, and We keep personally identifiable information such as Your name and email address so We can track Our communications with You and provide a high-quality service.

10. Complaints

When We receive a complaint We record all the information You have given Us and use it to try to resolve the complaint. If resolving Your complaint reasonably requires Us to contact someone else, We may share some of that information with them - this is a matter for Our sole discretion. We may also compile statistics from complaints to assess service levels, without identifying You or any other person.

If a dispute isn’t settled, We hope You will engage with Us in good-faith mediation. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint - though We’d appreciate the chance to discuss Your concern with You first.

11. Affiliate and Business Partner Information

Information given to Us in Your capacity as an affiliate or business partner is used to recognise visitors You have referred to Us and to credit and transfer any commission due. It is not used for any other purpose, and We undertake to preserve its confidentiality - We expect any affiliate or partner to reciprocate.

12. Cookies and Similar Storage

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. A website can also keep small items in your browser’s local storage, which work in a similar way. This section sets out exactly what our website stores and why.
Storage the site needs to work. Our website keeps two items in your browser’s local storage, and does not need your consent for them: “digiblu_consent”, which records the choices you make in our cookie banner so we do not ask you again (kept for six months); and “digiblu-theme”, which remembers whether you chose the light or dark look (set only if you use the theme button, and kept until you clear it). Neither is a cookie, neither tracks you, and neither is shared with anyone.
Analytics cookies, only with your permission. If you allow Analytics in our cookie banner, we use Google Analytics 4, a service provided by Google, to see which parts of the site are read, how people arrive and how many send an enquiry, so that we can improve it. When you send an enquiry, Google Analytics is told only that one was sent, never what you wrote. Google Analytics then sets two cookies: “_ga”, which tells one visitor’s browser apart from another so that visits can be counted, and “_ga_RVNLDVSLJ8”, which keeps the state of the current visit. Both are kept for up to two years. They do not identify you to us. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy, at policies.google.com/privacy. We have not switched on Google’s advertising features, and we do not share this data with other Google products.
Nothing loads until you choose. When you first visit, our cookie banner asks whether you allow Analytics. Until you answer, and if you reject, no Google code runs and no analytics cookie is set. Everything on the site works the same whether you accept or reject.
Changing your mind. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from “Cookie settings” in the footer of every page. Withdrawing Analytics stops Google Analytics immediately and deletes its cookies. Your browser also lets you view, block or delete cookies and local storage; if you clear them, we will ask you again on your next visit.
No advertising or marketing cookies. We do not use cookies for advertising, retargeting, social media tracking or profiling, and this website has no live chat, surveys or sign-in.
Our contact form. When you send an enquiry, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that the request comes from a person rather than an automated script. It runs inside the form, uses signals about your browser that are strictly necessary for that check, and sets no cookies on our site; we do not use its pre-clearance feature. Cloudflare describes what it processes at cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy. The details you enter are sent to us by email through Azure Communication Services, an email service provided by Microsoft, which processes them on our behalf to deliver that email to the people at DigiBlu who handle enquiries. Microsoft describes how it handles data at privacy.microsoft.com. We use your details to respond to your enquiry and for nothing else.

13. Personal Identifiers from Your Browsing Activity

Each request your browser makes for a page or a file passes through our hosting provider, Cloudflare, which processes your IP address and the details of your browser and device in order to deliver the site, keep it secure and protect it from abuse. We may look at Cloudflare’s aggregate logs to check that the site is running and to investigate problems. We do not use this information to identify or profile individual visitors. Any measurement of how the site is used happens only through Google Analytics, and only with your permission, as described in section 12.

14. Information We Obtain from Third Parties

We do not disclose Your personal information to third parties except as set out in this notice. We sometimes receive data indirectly derived from Your personal information via third parties whose services We use - none of it is personally identifiable to You.

15. Data May Be Processed Outside the European Union

We may use outsourced services outside the EU or EEA in Our business, so data could be processed outside the European Union. Where this happens, We require that data processing standards meet or exceed EU/EEA requirements, and that the processor follows the same binding corporate rules on data processing.

16. Your Duty to Inform Us of Changes

It’s important the personal data We hold about You is accurate and current - please keep Us informed if Your personal data changes.

17. Access to Your Personal Information

You may review or update personally identifiable information We hold about You at any time. To obtain a copy of information We hold, contact Us - We’ll tell You when to expect it and whether any fee applies.

18. Removal of Your Information

You can ask Us to remove personally identifiable information by contacting Us. Doing so may limit the service We’re able to provide You.

19. Verification of Your Information

When We receive a request to access, edit or delete personal identifiable information, We first take reasonable steps to verify Your identity before granting access or taking any action, to safeguard Your information.

20. Encryption of Data Sent Between Us

Where appropriate We use Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) certificates to verify Our identity to Your browser and encrypt data You give Us. You can check a transfer is using SSL by looking for a closed padlock symbol or other trust mark in Your browser’s address bar.

21. Retention Period for Personal Data

Except as otherwise stated in this policy, We keep Your personal information only as long as required - to provide the services You’ve requested, to comply with the law (including tax authority requirements), or to support a claim or defence in court.

22. Compliance with the Law

This Privacy Policy is compiled to comply with the law of every country or jurisdiction in which We aim to do business. If You believe it fails to satisfy the law of Your jurisdiction, please let Us know - ultimately, it’s Your choice whether to use Our website.

23. Review of This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The version that applies to You is the one posted on Our website on the day You use it. Updates may be made at any time without prior notice. Questions can be sent to privacy@digiblu.com for the attention of Our Data Protection and Privacy Officer, David Williams.
